Section 1 — Data & Documents
This is usually where the most time "disappears" — copying, pasting, formatting. ✍
Process 01
You manually copy data between systems
E.g. copying orders from your e-commerce platform to a spreadsheet, entering form data into your CRM, updating invoices based on an Excel table.
✓ Full automation possible ⏱ 1–2 weeks
Process 02
You create reports and summaries manually
Weekly sales report, team hours summary, monthly client update — always done manually, always from scratch.
✓ Automatic report generation ⏱ 1–3 weeks
Process 03
You send the same documents to every client individually
Contracts, invoices, briefs, proposals — you copy, update the client's details, and send. Every. Single. Time.
✓ Auto document generation & delivery ⏱ 1–2 weeks
If you checked 2+ items here → estimated time saved: 3–6 hours/week
Section 2 — Communication & Follow-ups
Repetitive messages are silent time thieves. 📨
Process 04
You manually send reminders to clients
Payment reminders, meeting confirmations, deadline alerts, subscription renewals — you write or copy-paste them every time.
✓ Event-triggered automations ⏱ 1–2 weeks
Process 05
You answer the same questions over and over
"What are your prices?", "How long does it take?", "Can I order X?" — answers are nearly identical every time.
✓ AI agent or FAQ automation ⏱ 1–3 weeks
Process 06
Client onboarding happens through copy-paste
You welcome every new client with the same message, send the same instructions, fill in the same spreadsheet — every time from scratch.
✓ Automated onboarding flow ⏱ 2–3 weeks
If you checked 2+ items here → estimated time saved: 2–5 hours/week
Section 3 — Operations
The heart of your business — often also the biggest mess. ⚙️
Process 07
You manually aggregate data from forms
Clients fill out contact forms, surveys, sign-up forms — and you or a team member manually transfers that data to a spreadsheet or system.
✓ Automatic form-to-system transfer ⏱ a few days
Process 08
You update statuses in multiple places separately
Changing an order status means updating your shop, spreadsheet, CRM, and sending a client message — all separately, all manually.
✓ One update → sync everywhere ⏱ 1–2 weeks
Process 09
You generate invoices or contracts manually
Open Word/Excel, type in client data and amount, save as PDF, send by email. Every time.
✓ Auto-generate & deliver instantly ⏱ 1–2 weeks
Process 10
Your tools don't talk to each other
You have a CRM, spreadsheets, invoicing software, a messaging app, an e-commerce platform — and none of them are connected. You transfer data by hand.
✓ API & platform integration (n8n) ⏱ 2–4 weeks
If you checked 3+ items here → this is likely your biggest automation opportunity.
